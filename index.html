Privacy Policy

At ChainRaise, your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information Collection:

We may collect minimal personal data such as email addresses, wallet addresses, or device metadata, strictly to improve the app experience.

2. Wallet Addresses:

We store your wallet address only to associate transactions or display related activity. We do not access or store private keys.

3. Usage Analytics:

We may use anonymous usage data to help us understand app performance and improve features. No personally identifiable information is collected.

4. Data Sharing:

We do not share your personal data with third parties unless required by law or with your consent.

5. Data Security:

We use best practices to protect your information and prevent unauthorized access.

6. Your Control:

You may delete your account or contact us to have your data removed from our servers.

7. Policy Updates:

This privacy policy may change over time. We will notify users of significant changes.

For questions or concerns, please contact us at support@chainraise.app.
